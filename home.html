About Us

When a vegetarian walks into a restaurant, the first challenge is often simply finding what they can eat. Most menus in the US are dominated by meat-based dishes, forcing vegetarians to scroll through long lists just to identify the few options available.

This webpage curates only the vegetarian dishes from each restaurant’s menu, making it easy for vegetarians to quickly spot, choose, and enjoy the food that fits their diet.

The page currently features a selection of restaurants around Princeton, New Jersey, with many more locations to be added soon.
